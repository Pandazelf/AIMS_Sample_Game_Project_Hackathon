=== EDUCATIONAL FOCUS ===

Game Context:
"Vietnam Flood Awareness Game - Focus on Mekong Delta flood preparedness, real-world scenarios based on 2024 flood disasters, educational flood safety for Vietnamese communities"

Target Learning Outcomes:
- Essential emergency supplies identification
- Evacuation timing and procedures
- Water contamination risks
- Community solidarity importance
- Resource management under crisis
- Medical emergency response
- Rescue signaling techniques
- Psychological resilience during disasters

=== GAME MECHANICS DECISIONS ===

Core Mechanics:
- 60-second item collection phase (tension building)
- 8-item backpack limit (forces prioritization)
- 14 available items (8 consumable, 6 non-consumable)
- 10 random scenarios per playthrough (from pool of 20)
- 3-4 choices per scenario (varying item requirements)
- Dynamic choice availability based on inventory
- Real-time item consumption during gameplay
- 5-star rating based on safe decisions (10/10 = perfect)

Educational Philosophy:
"Every choice should teach a real flood safety lesson. Consequences should be realistic but not preachy. Players learn through experiencing outcomes."
