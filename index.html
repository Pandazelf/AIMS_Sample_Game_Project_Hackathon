📖 About This Game

Every year, floods devastate the Mekong Delta region of Vietnam, affecting millions of families. In 2024 alone, floods caused billions of Vietnamese Dong in damage and displaced thousands.

Learn critical flood survival skills:

  • 🎒 What to pack in an emergency
  • 🚨 How to respond to flood warnings
  • 💧 Avoiding contaminated water
  • 🤝 Community solidarity during crisis
  • ⚡ Electrical hazards in floods

⚠️ Based on real flood preparedness guidelines from Vietnamese Red Cross

📋 How To Play / Cách Chơi

1

⏰ Phase 1: Preparation (60 seconds)

Flood warning issued! You have 60 seconds to grab items from your house.

Click on 8 items to add them to your emergency backpack.

💡 Choose wisely - you can't carry everything!

2

🌊 Phase 2: Flood Crisis (10 from 20 different scenarios)

Face 10 dangerous flood situations.

Make decisions based on the items you collected.

💡 The right items help you make safe choices!

3

⭐ Phase 3: Results

See how well you survived and learn real flood safety tips.

💡 Play again to improve your score!

Essential Flood Safety Rules:

  • ✅ Evacuate immediately when warned by authorities
  • ✅ Never walk through moving water (15cm can knock you down)
  • ✅ Avoid floodwater - it's contaminated
  • ✅ Turn off electricity before evacuating
  • ✅ Keep emergency supplies in a waterproof bag
  • ✅ Help elderly and disabled neighbors evacuate
